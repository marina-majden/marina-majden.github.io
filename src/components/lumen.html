03 — Process

Setup in four minutes,
smart in seven days.

Step 01

Screw & Connect

Replace any standard E27 or GU10 bulb. LUMEN connects to your Wi-Fi automatically. No hub required.

Step 02

Open the App

Rooms auto-discover. Name them. The app runs entirely on your local network — offline capable from day one.

Step 03

Pick a Scene

Choose from curated presets or build your own. Set schedules, moods, and wake-up routines in seconds.

Step 04

LUMEN Learns

After one week of observation, the AI adapts your lights automatically. You just live. It handles the rest.
